# AssessmentTracker Development — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026

> Published from AssessmentTracker, Effective Date September 27, 2026.

## Scope

This policy describes how AssessmentTracker Development handles information when used as a private, internal application for Southwest Trailer Service operational purposes. The application is limited to authorized operators and systems and is not a public consumer service.

## Data accessed

AssessmentTracker Development may access QuickBooks Online accounting and business information authorized by Southwest Trailer Service. The application accesses information needed for its internal operational functions through the QuickBooks Accounting scope. It does not use Intuit Payments.

## How data is used

Authorized information may be used for:

- internal operational reporting;
- reconciliation;
- workflow and automation support; and
- analysis needed to operate AssessmentTracker.

## Authorization and access controls

QuickBooks Online access occurs through Intuit OAuth. Access may be disconnected or revoked through the applicable Intuit or QuickBooks authorization controls.

Access to AssessmentTracker Development and the information it handles is limited to authorized operators and systems. On Development machines, the application enforces read-only QuickBooks access.

## Credentials and OAuth tokens

Application secrets and OAuth token state are intended to remain outside source control. The current Development architecture stores QuickBooks OAuth credentials and token state on controlled machine-local storage, rather than in Git or OneDrive.

## Sharing

Information handled by AssessmentTracker Development is not sold to advertisers. It is not intentionally provided to unrelated third parties, except to service providers or platforms needed to operate the authorized integration.

## Retention and deletion

Operational data and supporting evidence may be retained as needed for internal operational, audit, and reconciliation purposes. They may be removed when no longer required under the operator's applicable processes. This policy does not establish a fixed retention period.

## This policy page

This policy page requires no JavaScript and intentionally adds no tracking or cookies.
