# AssessmentTracker Development — Terms of Use

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026

> Published from AssessmentTracker, Effective Date September 27, 2026.

## Purpose and access

AssessmentTracker Development is a private, internal application used for Southwest Trailer Service operational purposes. It is intended only for operators who have been authorized to use it and to access the connected business systems.

AssessmentTracker Development is not offered for public sale and is not a general consumer service.

## Permitted use

Authorized users may use AssessmentTracker Development for internal business and operational work, including reporting, reconciliation, workflow support, and operational analysis.

Users must not:

- use the application without authorization;
- attempt to access data or systems beyond the access granted to them;
- share access with an unauthorized person; or
- use the application for an unrelated or unlawful purpose.

## QuickBooks Online connection

AssessmentTracker Development connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit OAuth and uses the QuickBooks Accounting scope. It does not use Intuit Payments.

On Development machines, the application enforces read-only QuickBooks access. Authorization to connect a QuickBooks company must be provided through the applicable Intuit authorization process.

## Internal software

AssessmentTracker Development is internal software and may be changed, suspended, or withdrawn as operational needs change. It is provided for authorized operational use, and users should independently review outputs before relying on them for business decisions.
